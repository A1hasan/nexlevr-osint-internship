## 2. Origin Story & Growth Timeline
* **2011 — Founding & German Döner Positioning:**  
  According to the brand's official story, Dade launched in Duhok in 2011 as the first authentic German-style döner kebab restaurant in Kurdistan, highlighting German culinary expertise, fresh meat, daily baked bread, and house-made sauces.
* **2016–2019 — Early Popularity & Operational Hurdles:**  
  Public Google Maps reviews dating back 9–10 years confirm Dade became a crowded, well-known local fast-food and family dining spot in Duhok. One historical review from ~7 years ago notes a temporary closure by the Duhok Health Department over cleanliness, after which the restaurant resumed operations and continued serving customers.
* **January – May 2021 — Formal Social Media Expansion:**  
  Meta transparency logs show the business formalized its social media presence in early 2021—joining Instagram in **January 2021** (`@daderestaurant`) and creating its official Facebook page (`Dade rest`) on **May 12, 2021** with zero subsequent page renames or merges.
* **2022–2026 — Co-Branding (`Babo & Dade`) & Digital Menu Modernization:**  
  Instagram bio data indicates joint branding with **Babo & Dade rest** alongside two separate Duhok contact lines. By 2026, the restaurant also deployed a mobile-friendly, trilingual web menu (`daderestaurant.vercel.app`, built by `@dlgash_5`) supporting 24/7 ordering and dining.
